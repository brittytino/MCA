🍲 Nutrition Tips

  • Include traditional millets like kambu, thinai, varagu in your meals.
  • Stay hydrated with buttermilk, coconut water, and herbal drinks.
  • Use cold-pressed oils like sesame and groundnut for cooking.
  • Eat seasonal vegetables like murungai keerai and vazhaipoo.

🏋️‍♂️ Workout Tips

  • Start with 5–10 mins of warm-up before any physical activity.
  • Practice Surya Namaskar or traditional stretches daily.
  • Incorporate walking, cycling, or bodyweight exercises.
  • Be consistent – small daily efforts add up.

🧘 Lifestyle Tips

  • Sleep by 10 PM and aim for 7–8 hours of rest.
  • Practice daily meditation or deep breathing for stress relief.
  • Limit screen time and engage in real-world activities.
  • Follow early dinners – a cultural tradition with health benefits.
